## Left as a conflict

The MUP address-procedure page still said **3 days** for temporary stay on 20 Nov 2026. The wizard follows the statute and keeps the page as a conflicting reading in `data/uncertainty.json`.

## Not changed

- Start of work still waits for a positive decision. NN 55/2026 renumbered Art. 91. The older sentence that allowed work on an employer certificate, after HZMO and HZZO registration, was not reprinted in the amendment text opened here.
- The euro range for working outside the inscribed area was not restated in that amendment text, so it is not printed in the wizard.
- Not re-verified from a page opened in this pass, and therefore not added: an HZZ 15-day answer, Art. 100.a caps, the zadužnica amount, the minimum wage, and the 16.5% health contribution.
- Form 18a is a visa-regime medical form. US citizens are not given it as a required check.
- Petrinjska 30 stays in the address book because the 2023 biometric notice still says so. The current how-and-where page does not list it and puts the foreigner card at Remetinečki gaj 13. The office note says to re-check before a visit.
- Hours on the how-and-where page and a later mirror disagree. The wizard does not freeze an hour.
